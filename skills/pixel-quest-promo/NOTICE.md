# Notices

Pixel Quest Promo is a Lookbk skill. The template, the 5×7 pixel font, the
hero sprites, power-ups, coins, blocks and scenery (all drawn as text grids
and turned into SVG in code), the scripts, the examples and this
documentation were written for Lookbk. It ships no images, no fonts, no
audio and no third-party code, and nothing in it was made with AI image
generation. The art is made in the spirit of 8-bit platform games and copies
no game's sprites, characters or logos.

It runs on third-party software that is fetched when you use it, not shipped
in this package:

- **HyperFrames** (`hyperframes@0.8.192`), Apache License 2.0,
  © 2026 HeyGen, Inc. https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes
  Run through `npx`; it renders the composition.
- **GSAP** 3.14.2, loaded by `template/index.html` from cdn.jsdelivr.net
  under the GSAP Standard License. https://gsap.com/standard-license
- **FFmpeg**, already installed on your machine, used by HyperFrames to
  encode the MP4 (and by you, if you add music as SKILL.md shows).

The products, features, numbers and links in `examples/` are made up.
