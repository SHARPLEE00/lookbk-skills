We gave ourselves five working days to replace three button libraries, two
colour scales and a spacing system nobody could explain. This is what we cut,
what we kept, and the one rule that made the week possible: nothing new gets
added until something old is deleted.

## Monday: count everything

Before touching a single component we listed every colour, size and radius
in production. There were 41 greys.
