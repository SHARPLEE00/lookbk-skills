The best dashboard we ever built had four numbers on it, no charts, and a
sentence under each number saying what to do when it moved. People opened it
every morning. The beautiful one with forty charts was opened by the person
who built it.

A dashboard is a promise that someone will act on what it shows. If nobody
knows what to do when a line goes up, the line is decoration. Start from the
decision, write it down, and only then pick the number that tells you when to
make it. Most of the time that number fits in a sentence, and the sentence is
more useful than the chart.
